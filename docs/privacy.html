Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

YouTube OAuth Manager is a desktop application that allows users to authorize access to their Google and YouTube accounts for local account management and export of OAuth credentials. This document describes the intended processing of data by the desktop utility.

Information handled

The utility requests authorized Google account email addresses, Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, and YouTube channel information through Google APIs. The user chooses whether to use features that act on their YouTube account.

Use of information

Account details identify the account the user has authorized. OAuth tokens enable API requests with the permissions approved by the user, including YouTube account operations and, when requested, subscribed channel actions.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and account information are stored locally on the user's device by the app; tokens may also be exported as plain-text files at the user's request. Exported tokens are sensitive and should be protected. The utility does not intentionally send tokens to a developer-operated account server. Google's own services process data when OAuth and YouTube API requests are made.

Control and deletion

Users may remove locally authorized accounts from the utility and revoke access in their Google Account at Google Account connections. Removing an account from the application does not necessarily invalidate already exported tokens; revoking access in Google is recommended.

Third-party services

Google and YouTube are governed by their own privacy policies and terms. This application is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
